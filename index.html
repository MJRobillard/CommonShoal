Why it matters

NOAA maps the past. You map the present.

Official charts update on a survey's schedule, not the tide's. Sandbars on the Tred Avon & Choptank shift with every strong blow, leaving keels and kayak hulls vulnerable alike. Every time you play the game, your track keeps the fleet, paddlers included, safe.

Groundings per year

U.S. Coast Guard, nationwide recreational boating accidents by type. Source: 2023 USCG Recreational Boating Statistics.

NOAA already runs a water-level monitor at this river's mouth and named the Choptank complex a federal Habitat Focus Area back in 2014. A live, crowdsourced shoal map is exactly the kind of dataset that effort can't generate on its own, but could absolutely put to use.
